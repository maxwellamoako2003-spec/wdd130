Ghana

Ghana is a beautiful country located in West Africa, known for its rich culture, history, and warm hospitality. It gained independence from British colonial rule on March 6, 1957, becoming the first sub-Saharan African country to gain independence. Ghana is famous for its traditional festivals, colorful kente cloth, delicious foods such as fufu and jollof rice, and beautiful tourist attractions like Cape Coast Castle, Kakum National Park, and Lake Volta. Its capital city is Accra, and its people are known for their friendliness, unity, and strong cultural values. Ghana is truly a special place with a proud history and a promising future.
